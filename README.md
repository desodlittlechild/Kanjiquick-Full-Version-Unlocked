## Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

KanjiQuick is a comprehensive tool designed to help users learn Japanese by recognizing characters visually and translating between English and Japanese. This software is particularly beneficial for those unfamiliar with the language, providing a user-friendly platform to explore over 6,500 Kanji characters, 47,000 compound words, and 80,000 names and places. Compared to typical dictionaries that may not cater to beginners, KanjiQuick offers an engaging way to familiarize yourself with Japanese symbols and their meanings.
